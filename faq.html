What is Loyalty Pass?

Loyalty Pass is a digital pass determined by the amount of RM the user is holding.

Why are only 50,000 Legend Passes available?

Allocation is below the current Mining Race Wallet user base of over 120,000 users, making it available in limited supply from launch.

How does the loyalty protocol work?

Mining Race Wallet checks your blockchain address for how much RM you have on a daily basis. Depending on the amount of RM in your Wallet, the system automatically activates the relevant Wallet Pass and its benefits in a fully decentralized manner. There is no need to manually activate your Wallet Pass since it will be updated depending on your RM holdings in real time.

Why is the supply limited?

A limited supply encourages early adoption while maintaining the value of the loyalty program for existing holders. Furthermore, the fixed supply allows partners to provide maximal loyalty program benefits for all participants.

Can more Passes be issued later?

The protocol has a set supply that cannot be exceeded, and there are no plans to issue additional passes beyond the initial allocation.

Why buy early instead of waiting?

The loyalty program contribution amount for the first 250K RM is the lowest possible compared to future allocations, providing the same utility and benefits. The longer you wait for the next allocation tier, the higher the contribution for the same amount of RM you will have to make.

What is a Loyalty Pass valuable for?

Loyalty Pass provides discounts and benefits for users, partners, and additional exclusive services within the Mining Race Wallet ecosystem. The value is in the perks offered as well as future wallet updates.

Is the Loyalty Pass an investment?

No. The Loyalty Pass is a product that provides discounts and benefits. It does not offer any sort of ownership, dividends, revenues, profit participation, or future value guarantees.

Can I save more than I spend?

Yes. Depending on how much you purchase within the ecosystem, your savings can potentially exceed your initial spending.

Can I save even more as time goes by?

Yes. Your savings can increase as you reach higher Spot levels, Hash Price, Mining Card price, buy more products with a discount, and as additional partners join the loyalty program.

Will all products be available for purchase with a discount?

Mining Race may add additional products to the list of eligible items over time. As new products become available, they will be added to the list of those that can be purchased with a discount.

Will all future products be eligible?

Mining Race may expand the list of eligible products over time. New products and promotions will be announced as they become available.

Can new features be added to the protocol?

Yes. Additional benefits, partners, and eligibility items can be added to the protocol over time with the same supply.

Why is the protocol launched after achieving 120,000+ users and tens of millions of blockchain transactions instead of before?

The ecosystem was created before the launch of the loyalty protocol. The protocol's utility serves to reward an existing active user base that spends within the ecosystem and offers them benefits from day one.

Is participation in the protocol mandatory?

The Loyalty Pass is optional. We encourage all our users to participate in the new program and enjoy all the benefits it brings. However, it is not mandatory.

Who owns the Protocol?

Mining Race develops and operates the ecosystem's wallet and products. The RM Foundation oversees the issuance of RM and is responsible for the protocol's rules, maintenance, and treasury management. Mining Race is the founding ecosystem that utilizes the protocol, whereas the Foundation is a separate organization that ensures the protocol's independence and sustainability.

Can I transfer my Pass to someone else?

Yes. Transfers to other users will be eligible after the protocol's full allocation or within a six-month period.

Why can't I transfer my Pass right away?

Transfers will be gradually enabled to ensure fair allocation of the initial supply among all users.

Why is there a 1% transfer fee?

Every transfer helps fund the Loyalty Treasury to provide more benefits and discounts for all Pass holders.

Will there be any changes once the supply reaches its limit?

After full allocation, there will be no additional passes available for purchase. After the initial six-month period, transfers will be enabled, and all new users will receive passes from existing holders.

What if demand for the pass exceeds the available supply?

Once the protocol reaches its maximum supply, there will be no further passes available.

How can Mining Race afford to provide such discounts?

Mining Race provides discounts thanks to the existing and prospective partner ecosystem, as well as the future value of the loyalty treasury.

Can the discounts provided by individual partners change?

Individual partner discounts may fluctuate; however, the general trend is one of continuous expansion in benefits and added value for Pass holders.

Why should I buy a Pass if I am not sure about my future purchases?

A Pass is ideal for long-term community members who plan to utilize the full potential of the Mining Race Wallet. A Pass entitles you to exclusive wallet features, partner discounts, and periodic rewards as a long-term member of the Mining Race Wallet community. The more you spend within the ecosystem, the more benefits you get over time.

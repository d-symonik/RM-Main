Premium Wallet Features.

The Mining Race Wallet is more than storage, it's the gateway to the RM ecosystem. Eligible Loyalty Pass holders unlock premium features that enhance convenience, security, and everyday use.

Safe Send

No manual wallet addresses. No copy-and-paste mistakes.

How It Works

  1. Enter the recipient's email address
  2. The recipient receives a confirmation request in their Mining Race Wallet
  3. They confirm the wallet linked to their account as the destination
  4. Enter the amount and send. The verified wallet address is used automatically behind the scenes

Smart Invoice

Create, send, and settle invoices directly from your wallet.

How It Works

  1. Create an invoice with the amount and payment details
  2. Send it to the recipient's email
  3. The recipient opens the invoice and taps Pay. Payment is sent automatically to the verified wallet
  4. The invoice is marked Settled and recorded in the Transaction Centre

Every payment is linked to the right invoice, the right recipient, and a transaction record.

Trusted Contacts

Save verified wallet addresses for secure, repeat transfers.

How It Works

  1. Add a contact using their email in address book
  2. The contact verifies their wallet
  3. Their verified wallet is saved to your Trusted Contacts
  4. Before each transfer, the contact confirms the destination wallet
  5. Enter the amount and send

Introduction

RM Foundation ("RM Foundation," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Loyalty Program Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you participate in the RM Foundation Loyalty Program and any related rewards, campaigns, memberships, promotions, or services (collectively, the "Loyalty Program").

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Loyalty Program. If you use the RM Foundation non-custodial wallet, the collection and processing of information relating specifically to the wallet is governed by the separate Wallet Privacy Policy.

By participating in the Loyalty Program, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all participants in the Loyalty Program worldwide, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your participation in the Loyalty Program, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country or region of residence
  • Account identifier
  • Membership information
  • Loyalty status
  • Membership tier
  • Reward points
  • Reward balances
  • Reward redemption history
  • Referral activity
  • Campaign participation
  • Promotional preferences
  • Customer support communications
  • Information voluntarily submitted through forms, surveys, or promotions

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Loyalty Program, we may automatically collect device information, operating system, browser type, IP address, app version, login timestamps, usage statistics, security logs, crash reports, and fraud prevention information.

4. How We Use Information

We may use your information to operate and administer the Loyalty Program, calculate rewards and benefits, verify eligibility, process redemptions, manage membership tiers, improve the Loyalty Program, detect fraud, respond to support requests, send service and promotional communications where permitted, analyze performance, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

5. Wallet Services

Participation in the Loyalty Program may be linked to the RM Foundation non-custodial wallet. Information relating specifically to wallet creation, wallet security, blockchain transactions, private keys, Secret Recovery Phrases, and other wallet functionality is governed by the separate Wallet Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The Loyalty Program may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, improve functionality, analyze usage, measure campaign performance, improve security, and detect fraudulent activity.

7. Sharing Information

We may share information with technology and infrastructure providers, cloud hosting providers, analytics providers, customer support providers, payment service providers, identity verification providers where applicable, professional advisers, and government authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in multiple jurisdictions where necessary to operate the Loyalty Program and supporting infrastructure, subject to applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Loyalty Program, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where applicable. Certain requests may be limited where we are legally required to retain information.

12. Marketing Communications

We may send Loyalty Program updates, reward notifications, campaign announcements, promotional offers, and service or security notifications. You may opt out of promotional communications at any time; essential service communications may still be sent.

13. Children's Privacy

The Loyalty Program is not intended for individuals under the age of 18 or the minimum legal age applicable in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Third-Party Services

The Loyalty Program may integrate with third-party services. RM Foundation is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services, and users should review their privacy policies.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version becomes effective upon publication unless otherwise stated. Continued participation constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

16. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official RM Foundation support channels.

17. Governing Principles

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the RM Foundation Loyalty Program Terms & Conditions and any other applicable policies. Where a separate policy applies to a specific RM Foundation product or service, including the Wallet Privacy Policy, that policy shall govern the processing of information relating to that product or service.

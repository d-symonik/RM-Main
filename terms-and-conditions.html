1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose

The RM Loyalty Programme ("Programme") is a blockchain-based customer loyalty programme established to reward long-term participation within the Mining Race Wallet ecosystem.

The Programme enables eligible participants to unlock Wallet Passes, loyalty discounts, exclusive product access, promotional opportunities, and additional ecosystem benefits based on the quantity of RM held within an eligible Mining Race Wallet.

The Programme is designed to encourage participation within the Mining Race Wallet ecosystem through utility-based benefits and is not intended to function as an investment programme, financial product, payment service, deposit programme, or securities offering.

1.2 Programme Operator

The Programme is operated by the RM Foundation.

Mining Race develops and operates the Mining Race Wallet, associated software, participating products, and integrated ecosystem services.

Mining Race acts as the founding ecosystem integrating the Programme into its Wallet and participating products.

The RM Foundation governs the Programme, including:

  • RM allocation
  • Programme Rules
  • Wallet Pass eligibility
  • Treasury administration
  • Programme governance
  • ecosystem expansion
  • future benefits
  • partner participation

The RM Foundation may appoint additional operators, administrators, contractors, affiliates or service providers to assist in operating the Programme.

1.3 Acceptance

By participating in the Programme, acquiring RM, holding RM within an eligible Mining Race Wallet, or using any Programme benefit, the participant confirms that they:

  • have read these Programme Rules;
  • understand these Programme Rules;
  • agree to be legally bound by these Programme Rules;
  • satisfy all eligibility requirements;
  • comply with all applicable laws.

Participation in the Programme is voluntary.

Participants may continue using Mining Race Wallet without participating in the Programme.

1.4 Programme Nature

The Programme is a customer loyalty programme.

Participation does not provide:

  • ownership of Mining Race;
  • ownership of the RM Foundation;
  • ownership of ecosystem assets;
  • ownership of Wallet software;
  • voting rights;
  • shareholder rights;
  • dividend rights;
  • profit participation;
  • revenue sharing;
  • debt claims;
  • repayment rights;
  • guaranteed value;
  • guaranteed appreciation;
  • guaranteed discounts;
  • guaranteed future benefits.

Any value derived from participation results solely from Programme utility, available benefits and participant use of the ecosystem.

1.5 Independent Programme

The Programme operates independently from:

  • Mining Grid products;
  • Mining services;
  • Wallet balances;
  • Digital asset prices;
  • Blockchain market values;
  • Cryptocurrency exchanges;
  • Financial markets.

Participation in the Programme does not require participants to engage in mining activities or purchase any Mining Grid products unless expressly stated for a specific promotion.

1.6 Programme Objectives

The Programme has been established to:

  • reward ecosystem participation;
  • encourage long-term Wallet usage;
  • strengthen partner relationships;
  • provide recurring loyalty benefits;
  • introduce exclusive Wallet functionality;
  • support ecosystem expansion;
  • create additional opportunities for participating members.

Benefits may evolve as the ecosystem expands.

1.7 Programme Flexibility

The Programme is intended to evolve over time.

Accordingly, the RM Foundation may introduce:

  • additional Wallet Pass levels;
  • new benefit categories;
  • new participating products;
  • new ecosystem services;
  • new partner programmes;
  • new promotional campaigns;
  • additional Wallet functionality;
  • new eligibility requirements;
  • updated verification procedures;
  • revised operational processes.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, such changes shall not increase the maximum RM allocation established by the Programme.

1.8 Fixed Allocation

The Programme is built upon a permanently limited RM allocation.

Unless these Programme Rules expressly provide otherwise:

  • no additional RM shall be created;
  • no additional Wallet Pass allocation shall be introduced beyond the maximum Programme allocation;
  • scarcity forms part of the Programme design.

Future Programme expansion shall occur through ecosystem growth rather than increasing RM allocation.

2. DEFINITIONS

Unless the context requires otherwise, the following capitalized terms shall have the meanings set out below.

2.1 Account

Account means a registered user account within the Mining Race Wallet ecosystem through which a Participant accesses Wallet services and the Programme.

2.2 Affiliate

Affiliate means any company, entity, subsidiary, parent company, contractor, joint venture, strategic partner, successor, or other organization that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or operates in cooperation with Mining Race or the RM Foundation.

2.3 Allocation

Allocation means the issuance or distribution of RM by the RM Foundation in accordance with these Programme Rules.

Each Allocation may have its own contribution amount, eligibility criteria, participation period, promotional conditions, or availability.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, Allocations are made on a first-come, first-served basis while available.

2.4 Benefits

Benefits means any feature, privilege, discount, promotion, utility, access right, service, Wallet functionality, reward, partner offer, recognition, or other advantage made available to eligible Participants through the Programme.

Benefits may vary by Wallet Pass, jurisdiction, product, promotion, participating partner, or other eligibility requirements.

Benefits have no monetary value unless expressly stated by the Programme.

2.5 Blockchain

Blockchain means the distributed ledger technology used to record RM ownership, transfers, and other supported Programme transactions.

The RM Foundation may determine the supported blockchain network(s) used by the Programme.

2.6 Contribution

Contribution means the amount required by the RM Foundation to obtain RM during a particular Allocation.

A Contribution is paid solely to obtain participation within the Programme and does not constitute an investment, deposit, savings product, security, or financial instrument.

2.7 Eligible Product

Eligible Product means any product, service, subscription, digital offering, Wallet functionality, partner product, or other item designated by the RM Foundation or Mining Race as eligible to receive Programme Benefits.

Eligibility may vary between products and may change from time to time.

2.8 Mining Race

Mining Race means Mining Race Wallet, and any of its Affiliates responsible for developing or operating the Wallet, ecosystem products, Wallet infrastructure, software, and participating services integrated with the Programme.

2.9 Participant

Participant means an individual or legal entity that satisfies the eligibility requirements of these Programme Rules and participates in the Programme.

2.10 Partner

Partner means any third-party organization approved by the RM Foundation to provide Benefits, products, services, discounts, or other Programme utilities.

Participation by a Partner is voluntary and subject to separate commercial arrangements.

2.11 Programme

Programme means the RM Loyalty Programme established and governed by these Programme Rules.

2.12 Programme Rules

Programme Rules means this document, together with any schedules, appendices, notices, amendments, policies, operational procedures, or Programme announcements published by the RM Foundation from time to time.

2.13 RM

RM means the blockchain-based digital loyalty unit issued by the RM Foundation as part of the Programme.

RM is used to determine Wallet Pass eligibility, access Programme Benefits, and participate in eligible ecosystem features and services made available by the RM Foundation, Mining Race, and participating Partners.

RM is not a share, security, debt instrument, investment contract, deposit, payment instrument, stablecoin, ownership interest, or other regulated financial product.

The acquisition of RM through an Allocation constitutes participation in the Programme in accordance with these Programme Rules. Any Contribution made to obtain RM is made solely for Programme participation and does not establish or guarantee the present or future value of RM, any resale price, market liquidity, appreciation, or the availability of any secondary market.

Except where expressly provided under these Programme Rules, RM does not entitle a Participant to dividends, profit sharing, revenue sharing, ownership rights, voting rights, or any claim against Mining Race, the RM Foundation, or any Affiliate.

2.14 RM Foundation

RM Foundation means the entity responsible for governing, administering, and maintaining the Programme, including RM Allocation, Programme Rules, Treasury management, Wallet Pass eligibility, and long-term Programme development.

2.15 Treasury

Treasury means the Programme reserve administered by the RM Foundation to support Programme operations, future Benefits, partner incentives, ecosystem initiatives, promotional campaigns, and other Programme purposes.

The Treasury may receive funding from transfer fees, partner contributions, promotional allocations, ecosystem revenues, or other approved sources.

2.16 Wallet

Wallet means a compatible Mining Race Wallet approved for participation in the Programme.

Only supported Wallets may be eligible to receive Programme Benefits.

2.17 Wallet Pass

Wallet Pass means the Participant's membership tier automatically assigned according to the quantity of RM held in an eligible Wallet.

Wallet Passes determine the level of Programme Benefits available to the Participant.

Wallet Passes are not separate digital assets and do not exist independently from RM holdings.

2.18 Wallet Pass Level

Wallet Pass Level means a specific membership tier, including Racer Pass, Champion Pass, Legend Pass, or any future Wallet Pass introduced by the RM Foundation.

Each Wallet Pass Level may provide different Benefits.

2.19 Plus Status

Plus Status means an enhanced recognition status earned through cumulative eligible participation while maintaining the required Wallet Pass level.

Plus Status provides additional Benefits independent of standard Wallet Pass Benefits.

Eligibility requirements for Plus Status shall be determined by the RM Foundation.

2.20 Transfer

Transfer means any movement of RM between eligible Wallets using supported Programme functionality.

Transfers may be subject to eligibility requirements, verification procedures, transfer fees, waiting periods, or other restrictions established by the RM Foundation.

2.21 Transfer Fee

Transfer Fee means the percentage deducted from an eligible Transfer in accordance with these Programme Rules.

Transfer Fees form part of the Programme's Treasury funding mechanism unless otherwise determined by the RM Foundation.

2.22 Verification

Verification means any identity, compliance, security, ownership, sanctions screening, anti-money laundering, fraud prevention, or other review process required before a Participant may access certain Programme features.

Verification requirements may change over time to comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, or Programme security standards.

3. PROGRAMME MEMBERSHIP

3.1 Membership

Participation in the Programme is voluntary.

Individuals and eligible legal entities that satisfy these Programme Rules may participate in the Programme by holding RM within an eligible Mining Race Wallet.

Membership is established automatically when a Participant satisfies all applicable eligibility requirements. No separate membership application or manual activation is required unless expressly specified by the RM Foundation.

Membership in the Programme does not create a partnership, agency, joint venture, employment relationship, fiduciary relationship, shareholder relationship, or ownership interest between the Participant and Mining Race, the RM Foundation, or any Affiliate.

3.2 Eligibility

To participate in the Programme, a Participant must:

a. maintain an eligible Mining Race Wallet;

b. satisfy the minimum Wallet Pass requirements established by the Programme;

c. comply with these Programme Rules;

d. comply with all applicable laws and regulations;

e. successfully complete any required verification procedures;

f. not be subject to sanctions, restrictions, or other legal prohibitions preventing participation.

The RM Foundation may establish additional eligibility requirements for specific Programme Benefits, promotions, products, jurisdictions, or Wallet features.

Participation may not be available in every jurisdiction.

3.3 Individual Participation

Unless otherwise approved by the RM Foundation, each Participant is responsible for maintaining and controlling their own Wallet.

The Participant represents and warrants that:

  • all information provided is accurate;
  • they have legal capacity to participate;
  • participation does not violate applicable law;
  • they are acting on their own behalf unless expressly authorized otherwise.

The Participant remains solely responsible for all activity conducted through their Wallet.

3.4 Business Participation

Legal entities may participate where permitted by the RM Foundation.

The RM Foundation may require:

  • corporate verification;
  • beneficial ownership information;
  • authorized representative documentation;
  • additional compliance procedures.

Business participation may be subject to separate operational requirements.

3.5 Membership Is Personal

Unless expressly permitted by these Programme Rules:

  • Programme Membership is personal to the eligible Wallet;
  • Membership may not be assigned;
  • Membership may not be pledged;
  • Membership may not be licensed;
  • Membership may not be rented;
  • Membership may not be sold independently of eligible RM Transfers.

Only RM may become transferable in accordance with these Programme Rules.

Wallet Passes themselves cannot be transferred independently because they exist solely as a reflection of eligible RM holdings.

3.6 Automatic Wallet Pass Recognition

Wallet Pass eligibility is determined automatically through supported Programme systems.

No manual request is required.

Wallet Pass status may change automatically whenever:

  • RM holdings increase;
  • RM holdings decrease;
  • Transfers occur;
  • compliance restrictions apply;
  • Programme Rules require an adjustment.

Participants acknowledge that Wallet Pass status is dynamic and may change without prior notice whenever eligibility requirements change.

3.7 Membership Duration

Membership continues while the Participant:

  • maintains an eligible Wallet;
  • satisfies applicable eligibility requirements;
  • complies with these Programme Rules.

Membership does not expire solely due to the passage of time unless otherwise specified for a particular Programme feature or promotional campaign.

3.8 No Guaranteed Membership

The RM Foundation reserves the right to refuse, suspend, restrict, or terminate participation where it reasonably determines that:

  • Programme abuse has occurred;
  • fraudulent activity is suspected;
  • verification requirements are not satisfied;
  • legal or regulatory obligations require action;
  • participation could expose the Programme or its Participants to unacceptable operational, legal, financial, or reputational risk.

3.9 Multiple Wallets

Participants may maintain multiple eligible Wallets unless prohibited by these Programme Rules or applicable law.

The RM Foundation may aggregate Wallets controlled by the same Participant for compliance, fraud prevention, verification, promotional eligibility, or operational purposes.

Where multiple Wallets are used to circumvent Programme Rules, promotional limits, transfer restrictions, or eligibility requirements, the RM Foundation may treat those Wallets as a single Participant.

3.10 Membership Communications

By participating in the Programme, Participants consent to receive Programme-related communications, including:

  • Wallet Pass updates;
  • Programme announcements;
  • operational notices;
  • security notifications;
  • promotional campaigns;
  • Benefit updates;
  • partner announcements;
  • amendments to these Programme Rules.

Participants may manage marketing communication preferences where such options are made available.

Operational, legal, compliance, and security communications may continue regardless of marketing preferences where required for the proper administration of the Programme.

3.11 No Property Right in Membership

Programme Membership is a contractual benefit provided under these Programme Rules.

Membership does not constitute property, a vested right, or an irrevocable entitlement.

Except where prohibited by applicable law, the RM Foundation may modify, suspend, or discontinue any aspect of the Programme in accordance with these Programme Rules.

3.12 Participant Responsibilities

Each Participant is responsible for:

  • safeguarding access to their Wallet;
  • maintaining accurate account information;
  • complying with applicable laws;
  • protecting Wallet credentials and recovery phrases;
  • reviewing Programme updates and amendments;
  • ensuring continued eligibility for Programme Benefits.

Failure to comply with these responsibilities may result in reduced Programme functionality, suspension of Benefits, or termination of Membership.

4. WALLET ELIGIBILITY

4.1 Eligible Wallet

Participation in the Programme requires the Participant to maintain an eligible Mining Race Wallet.

Only Wallets designated by the RM Foundation as compatible with the Programme may receive Wallet Pass recognition and Programme Benefits.

The RM Foundation may expand, restrict, or discontinue support for Wallets at its sole discretion.

4.2 Non-Custodial Wallet

The Mining Race Wallet is designed as a non-custodial wallet.

Participants retain sole responsibility for:

  • safeguarding their Wallet;
  • maintaining control of their private keys or recovery phrase;
  • authorizing transactions;
  • securing access credentials;
  • protecting their devices.

Neither Mining Race, the RM Foundation, nor any Affiliate has custody of Participant assets, recovery phrases, or private keys.

Accordingly, they are unable to recover lost Wallet access, reverse blockchain transactions, or restore Wallet ownership where control has been lost.

4.3 Wallet Ownership

Programme Benefits are determined solely by the quantity of eligible RM held within an eligible Wallet.

The Programme does not determine legal ownership of a Wallet or any digital asset.

Where ownership of a Wallet is disputed, the RM Foundation may suspend Programme Benefits until the dispute has been resolved to its reasonable satisfaction.

4.4 Wallet Verification

The RM Foundation may require verification of:

  • Wallet ownership;
  • Participant identity;
  • source of funds;
  • residency;
  • legal capacity;
  • beneficial ownership;
  • or any other information reasonably required for Programme administration or legal compliance.

Failure to complete required verification may result in:

  • suspension of Programme Benefits;
  • delayed Transfers;
  • restricted Wallet functionality;
  • termination of Programme participation.

Verification requirements may vary depending on jurisdiction, applicable law, the nature of the requested service, or the level of Programme participation.

4.5 Wallet Pass Recognition

Wallet Pass eligibility is determined automatically by supported Programme systems based on the quantity of eligible RM held within the Participant's eligible Wallet.

No manual application or activation is required.

Wallet Pass recognition is dynamic and may automatically change whenever:

  • RM holdings increase;
  • RM holdings decrease;
  • RM is Transferred;
  • Programme Rules are amended;
  • compliance restrictions apply.

Wallet Pass recognition reflects the Participant's current eligibility only and does not constitute a permanent status.

4.6 Snapshot Timing

The RM Foundation may determine Wallet Pass eligibility using one or more blockchain balance snapshots, live blockchain data, or other supported verification methods.

Programme Benefits may become available immediately or after a reasonable processing period, depending on operational requirements.

The timing of Wallet Pass recognition shall be determined solely by the Programme's operational procedures.

4.7 Supported Blockchain Networks

The Programme operates only on blockchain networks designated by the RM Foundation.

RM held on unsupported blockchain networks, unsupported Wallets, wrapped assets, bridged assets, or any unauthorized implementation may not qualify for Programme Benefits.

The RM Foundation has no obligation to recognize unsupported implementations.

4.8 Blockchain Confirmation

Programme actions may require sufficient blockchain confirmations before becoming effective.

Accordingly:

  • Wallet Pass updates;
  • Transfers;
  • Programme eligibility;
  • Wallet recognition;
  • and Benefits

may be delayed until the required confirmations have been completed.

The required number of confirmations may change from time to time.

The RM Foundation shall not be responsible for delays, interruptions, or changes resulting from:

  • blockchain congestion;
  • network outages;
  • validator failures;
  • forks;
  • chain reorganizations;
  • software bugs;
  • denial-of-service attacks;
  • cyber incidents;
  • unsupported protocol upgrades;
  • third-party infrastructure failures;
  • force majeure events.

4.10 Wallet Security

Participants remain solely responsible for protecting:

  • private keys;
  • recovery phrases;
  • passwords;
  • authentication credentials;
  • devices;
  • backups.

Loss of Wallet credentials may permanently prevent access to Programme Benefits.

Mining Race and the RM Foundation cannot regenerate, replace, recover, or reset recovery phrases or private keys.

4.11 Unsupported Transactions

Transactions involving:

  • unauthorized smart contracts;
  • unsupported Wallet software;
  • prohibited jurisdictions;
  • sanctioned addresses;
  • suspicious activity;
  • blockchain manipulation;
  • fraudulent transactions;

may be excluded from Programme eligibility.

The RM Foundation may disregard any transaction that it reasonably determines does not represent genuine Programme participation.

4.12 No Guarantee of Continuous Availability

The Mining Race Wallet and Programme are intended to operate continuously; however, uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.

Maintenance, upgrades, security enhancements, legal requirements, technical issues, or circumstances beyond the reasonable control of Mining Race or the RM Foundation may temporarily affect Wallet functionality or Programme availability.

Reasonable efforts will be made to restore normal operation as soon as practicable.

4.13 Future Wallet Features

The RM Foundation and Mining Race may introduce additional Wallet features, including new security mechanisms, supported networks, authentication methods, Wallet functionality, or eligibility requirements.

Participants may be required to update their Wallet software or complete additional verification to continue accessing certain Programme Benefits.

5. WALLET PASSES

5.1 Wallet Pass Programme

The Programme includes a tiered membership structure known as Wallet Passes.

Wallet Passes are designed to recognize eligible Participants based on the quantity of RM held within an eligible Wallet and determine eligibility for Programme Benefits.

Wallet Passes are automatically assigned and maintained by the Programme.

No manual application, approval, or activation is required unless expressly specified by the RM Foundation.

5.2 Wallet Pass Levels

At the Effective Date of these Programme Rules, the Programme includes the following Wallet Pass Levels:

  • Racer Pass
  • Champion Pass
  • Legend Pass

The RM Foundation may introduce additional Wallet Pass Levels, retire existing Wallet Pass Levels, rename Wallet Pass Levels, or revise eligibility requirements at its sole discretion.

The introduction of new Wallet Pass Levels shall not increase the maximum RM Allocation established by the Programme unless expressly approved under these Programme Rules.

5.3 Automatic Assignment

Wallet Pass Levels are assigned automatically based on the Participant's eligible RM holdings.

Assignment occurs through supported Programme systems without manual intervention.

Participants acknowledge that Wallet Pass recognition is dynamic and reflects their current eligibility at the relevant time.

5.4 Eligibility Thresholds

Each Wallet Pass Level requires the Participant to maintain the minimum quantity of RM determined by the RM Foundation.

Eligibility thresholds may be published through:

  • the Mining Race Wallet;
  • the Programme website;
  • official Programme documentation;
  • Programme announcements;
  • or other official communication channels.

The RM Foundation may revise Wallet Pass eligibility thresholds where reasonably necessary for Programme administration, provided such revisions do not increase the total maximum RM Allocation.

5.5 Upgrades

Where a Participant becomes eligible for a higher Wallet Pass Level, the Programme shall automatically recognize the upgraded Wallet Pass.

Unless otherwise specified:

  • upgraded Benefits become available following successful recognition;
  • promotional Benefits may be subject to separate eligibility conditions;
  • additional verification may be required for certain Benefits.

The timing of Wallet Pass upgrades shall be determined by the Programme's operational procedures.

5.6 Downgrades

If a Participant no longer satisfies the eligibility requirements for a Wallet Pass Level, the Programme may automatically assign the appropriate Wallet Pass corresponding to the Participant's current RM holdings.

Downgrades may occur immediately or following any grace period determined by the RM Foundation.

Benefits associated with the previous Wallet Pass may cease upon downgrade unless otherwise specified.

5.7 Wallet Pass Recognition

Wallet Pass recognition reflects only the Participant's eligibility under these Programme Rules.

Wallet Pass recognition does not constitute:

  • a property right;
  • a contractual guarantee of future Benefits;
  • a financial entitlement;
  • an investment interest;
  • ownership in the RM Foundation;
  • ownership in Mining Race.

Wallet Pass recognition may change whenever Programme eligibility changes.

5.8 Wallet Pass Benefits

Each Wallet Pass may provide access to different Programme Benefits, including but not limited to:

  • discounts;
  • promotional campaigns;
  • exclusive products;
  • early access opportunities;
  • partner offers;
  • Wallet functionality;
  • Plus Status eligibility;
  • priority access;
  • recognition features;
  • additional ecosystem utilities.

Benefits may differ between Wallet Pass Levels and jurisdictions.

Not every Benefit will be available to every Participant.

5.9 Promotional Wallet Pass Benefits

From time to time, the RM Foundation may introduce temporary Wallet Pass Benefits.

Temporary Benefits may include:

  • seasonal promotions;
  • launch campaigns;
  • partner promotions;
  • limited-time rewards;
  • bonus eligibility;
  • special recognition events.

Temporary Benefits automatically expire at the end of the applicable promotional period unless otherwise stated.

5.10 Wallet Pass Review

The RM Foundation may periodically review Wallet Pass eligibility to ensure ongoing compliance with these Programme Rules.

Such reviews may include:

  • blockchain verification;
  • Wallet ownership review;
  • compliance screening;
  • fraud detection;
  • eligibility confirmation.

Participants agree to cooperate with any reasonable review conducted by the RM Foundation.

5.11 No Guaranteed Benefits

Wallet Pass Levels provide eligibility to participate in Programme Benefits.

The RM Foundation does not guarantee:

  • the availability of any particular Benefit;
  • the continuation of any Benefit;
  • the value of any Benefit;
  • the frequency of promotions;
  • the availability of participating Partners.

Benefits may be added, modified, suspended, or withdrawn at any time in accordance with these Programme Rules.

5.12 Nature of Wallet Passes

Wallet Passes exist solely to determine a Participant's eligibility for Programme Benefits under these Programme Rules.

Wallet Passes:

  • are not separate stable digital assets;
  • are automatically assigned based on eligible RM holdings;
  • may not be sold, assigned, licensed, pledged, or otherwise transferred independently of eligible RM holdings;
  • do not constitute property, securities, investment interests, or ownership rights;
  • do not represent ownership in RM Foundation, any Affiliate, or any Programme assets.

Wallet Passes exist solely as a recognition mechanism within the Programme and do not exist independently from the Participant's eligible RM holdings.

5.13 Wallet Pass Misuse

The RM Foundation may suspend or revoke Wallet Pass recognition where it reasonably determines that a Participant has:

  • manipulated Programme eligibility;
  • attempted to artificially obtain Benefits;
  • engaged in fraudulent activity;
  • abused promotional campaigns;
  • violated these Programme Rules;
  • interfered with Programme operation.

Any decision under this Section shall be made acting reasonably and in accordance with applicable law.

5.14 Future Wallet Pass Development

The Programme is intended to evolve over time.

Accordingly, the RM Foundation may introduce:

  • additional Wallet Pass Levels;
  • invitation-only Wallet Passes;
  • institutional Wallet Passes;
  • partner Wallet Passes;
  • regional Wallet Passes;
  • premium Wallet Pass experiences;

Unless expressly stated otherwise, future Wallet Passes shall remain subject to these Programme Rules.

6. RM ALLOCATION

6.1 Fixed Programme Allocation

The Programme is established upon a permanently limited maximum allocation of RM (the "Maximum RM Allocation").

The Maximum RM Allocation shall be determined and published by the RM Foundation through the official Programme documentation.

Except as expressly provided under these Programme Rules, no additional RM shall be created or allocated beyond the published Maximum RM Allocation.

The Maximum RM Allocation forms a fundamental characteristic of the Programme.

6.2 Initial Allocation

The RM Foundation may conduct an Initial Allocation during which eligible Participants may obtain RM in accordance with these Programme Rules and the applicable Allocation Terms.

The Initial Allocation may include one or more participation phases, pricing structures, eligibility requirements, Wallet Pass availability, promotional benefits, or participation limits determined by the RM Foundation.

Participation shall remain subject to availability.

6.3 Future Allocations

Subject to the availability of unallocated RM within the Maximum RM Allocation, the RM Foundation may conduct one or more future Allocation events.

The decision to conduct, postpone, modify, or cancel any future Allocation shall be made by the RM Foundation acting reasonably and in accordance with applicable law.

Nothing in these Programme Rules creates any obligation upon the RM Foundation to conduct additional Allocation events.

6.4 Allocation Terms

Each Allocation may be governed by separate Allocation Terms published by the RM Foundation.

Allocation Terms may include, without limitation:

  • eligibility requirements;
  • participation periods;
  • Contribution Amounts;
  • Wallet Pass eligibility;
  • promotional benefits;
  • minimum or maximum participation amounts;
  • jurisdictional restrictions;
  • payment methods;
  • settlement procedures;
  • any other operational requirements.

Where an inconsistency exists between these Programme Rules and Allocation Terms, these Programme Rules shall prevail unless the Allocation Terms expressly state otherwise.

6.5 Contribution Amount

Each Allocation shall specify the applicable Contribution Amount required to obtain RM.

Contribution Amounts may vary between Allocation events.

A Contribution represents consideration paid to participate in the Programme and obtain the corresponding RM Allocation.

A Contribution does not constitute:

  • an investment;
  • a deposit;
  • a loan;
  • the purchase of securities;
  • the purchase of equity;
  • an expectation of financial return.

A Contribution does not establish or guarantee:

  • the present or future value of RM;
  • appreciation of RM;
  • future demand;
  • market liquidity;
  • resale opportunities;
  • availability of any secondary market.

6.6 Completion of Allocation

An Allocation shall be deemed completed once:

  • the applicable Contribution has been successfully received (where applicable);
  • all required verification procedures have been completed;
  • the corresponding RM has been allocated to the Participant's eligible Wallet.

Following completion, Wallet Pass eligibility shall be determined automatically under these Programme Rules.

6.7 Wallet Pass Eligibility Following Allocation

Obtaining RM through an Allocation does not guarantee continued eligibility for any Wallet Pass.

Wallet Pass recognition shall at all times depend upon the Participant maintaining the applicable quantity of eligible RM within an eligible Wallet.

Where RM holdings subsequently decrease, Wallet Pass recognition may automatically change in accordance with these Programme Rules.

6.8 Allocation Availability

All Allocations are limited.

Allocations may conclude before the scheduled closing date where the applicable Allocation has been fully subscribed.

The RM Foundation shall have no obligation to reopen or extend an Allocation after its conclusion.

6.9 Unallocated RM

Any RM that remains unallocated following an Allocation may, at the discretion of the RM Foundation, be:

  • reserved for future Allocation events;
  • allocated to the Programme Treasury;
  • reserved for ecosystem development;
  • reserved for strategic partnerships;
  • reserved for promotional campaigns;
  • permanently withheld from Allocation;
  • otherwise administered in a manner consistent with these Programme Rules.

Such administration shall not increase the Maximum RM Allocation.

6.10 Allocation Limits

The RM Foundation may establish reasonable limits applicable to any Allocation, including:

  • minimum participation amounts;
  • maximum participation amounts;
  • Participant limits;
  • Wallet limits;
  • jurisdictional limits;
  • promotional limits;
  • verification requirements.

Such limits may differ between Allocation events.

6.11 Jurisdiction Restrictions

Participation in an Allocation may not be available in every jurisdiction.

The RM Foundation may restrict or prohibit participation where required by:

  • applicable law;
  • regulatory requirements;
  • sanctions;
  • compliance obligations;
  • operational considerations;
  • risk management requirements.

Participants are solely responsible for determining whether participation is lawful in their jurisdiction.

6.12 Refusal of Participation

Subject to applicable law, the RM Foundation may refuse participation in an Allocation where it reasonably determines that:

  • eligibility requirements have not been satisfied;
  • required verification has not been completed;
  • sanctions restrictions apply;
  • fraudulent activity is suspected;
  • false information has been provided;
  • participation may expose the Programme to legal, regulatory, operational, financial, or reputational risk.

The RM Foundation shall not be required to disclose confidential compliance assessments supporting such decision.

6.13 Allocation Corrections

Where an Allocation has occurred as a result of:

  • technical malfunction;
  • blockchain error;
